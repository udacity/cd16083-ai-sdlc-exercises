# Score a Supplied Plan and Estimate

A developer at Alder Box asked an AI assistant to plan a renewal reminder feature and estimate it in days. The plan reads well. Your job is to score it against what the team actually knows, find what it got wrong, and write the decision record the team needs before anyone starts building.

## What you will produce

The completed `four-questions.md` and `decision-record.md`.

## Requirements

- Score rows 2, 3 and 4 of `four-questions.md`. Answer each row by finding something specific in the plan, and say what you found. Row 1 is worked as an example.
- Answer the line below the table: what data does the plan keep, for how long, and who decides?
- Fill in all five fields of `decision-record.md`: Decision, Alternative considered, Why it lost, Assumptions to confirm, and Who decides.

## Files

- `the-change.md`: what the team agreed to build
- `workflow-narrative.md`: the company and the team (read the first two sections and step 7)
- `supplied-plan.md`: the assistant's plan and estimate
- `four-questions.md`: the scoring table
- `decision-record.md`: the decision record template

## Tools

None. Nothing in this exercise is generated.
