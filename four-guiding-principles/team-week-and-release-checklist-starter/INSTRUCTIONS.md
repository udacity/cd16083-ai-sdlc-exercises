# Map the Team's Week and Direct the Release Checklist

Alder Box, a small book-subscription company, turned one customer email into a shipped feature in a week, using the same AI assistant at every step. The feature went wrong on Monday. Your job is to find where the team's oversight slipped, then direct the assistant better than the team did on the step that hurt most: the release checklist.

## What you will produce

The completed `worksheet.md`, Parts A, C, D and E. Part F is an optional challenge.

## Requirements

- **Part A:** For steps 6 and 7 of `workflow-narrative.md`, record what each output was checked against and what was available but not used. Then mark each of the four questions S, V or – using the rubric on the worksheet.
- **Part C:** Finish `scaffold.md`: complete the constraints and add two evaluation criteria of your own, written before you run anything. Run the scaffold in Claude Code and paste the checklist it returns.
- **Part D:** Name three oversight decisions the team made and what went wrong on Monday because of each. Add one sentence on why the team's checklist looked right and was not.
- **Part E:** Score your checklist against your three criteria. Then read Sam's prompt in `team-prompts.md` and write one sentence on what your direction gave the assistant that his did not.

Open `team-prompts.md` only when Part E asks for it.

## Files

- `workflow-narrative.md`: the team's week, step by step
- `release-checklist-team.md`: the sixty-item checklist the assistant gave the team
- `scaffold.md`: the four-part direction (context, constraints, examples, evaluation criteria) you complete
- `team-prompts.md`: the prompts the team typed
- `worksheet.md`: your answers

## Tools

Claude Code in the workspace, for Part C.
