# Requirements checklist

Six yes-or-no questions to ask of any set of user stories, with the scenario brief open beside
them. "Yes" means the set passes the item. For a "no", name the story or the gap.

| # | Question | What a "no" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does every user group in the brief have at least one story? | A user group named in the brief does not have a story. |
| 2 | Does every story have acceptance criteria someone could check? | A story has no criteria, or criteria that cannot be tested ("works well"). |
| 3 | Do the stories agree with each other? | Two stories contradict each other. |
| 4 | Does every story agree with the rules in the brief? | A story, or one of its criteria, contradicts a rule in the brief. |
| 5 | Does everything in the stories trace to the brief? | A story adds a feature, system, rule, or standard the brief does not ask for. |
| 6 | Is every rule and every existing system in the brief covered by at least one story? | A rule or system in the brief is not mentioned in any story. A mention counts, even inside one criterion, and even a wrong one (a wrong rule is item 4's problem; here the rule counts as covered). |

Items 5 and 6 are opposites: 5 catches what was added, 6 catches what was left out.
