# Score the Clinic's User Stories, Then Direct Your Own

Cedar Clinic, a three-location family practice, wants online appointment booking. A vendor supplied eight user stories that an AI assistant wrote from a one-line prompt. Your job is to score those stories against the clinic's brief, decide what may safely go into a prompt, direct the assistant to write better stories, and tell the practice manager whether either set can be relied on.

## What you will produce

The completed `worksheet.md`, Parts A, C, D and F.

## Requirements

- **Part A:** Score the supplied stories on checklist items 1, 4 and 6, each by finding something in the stories or the brief. Items 2, 3 and 5 are worked for you. Count the words in the prompt that produced the stories.
- **Part C:** For each of the four things someone might paste into a prompt, mark it allowed or not allowed and copy the words of `data-handling-policy.md` that decide it.
- **Part D:** Finish `scaffold-stories.md` (the constraints and two evaluation criteria), run it in Claude Code, and paste the stories with their "Assumptions to confirm" list. Score your stories on items 1, 4 and 6.
- **Part F:** Complete the written assessment rows for the stories, with a verdict for each set.

Optional: revise your direction once based on what Part D found, rerun it, and rescore.

## Files

- `scenario-brief.md`: the clinic, its users, its rules and its existing systems
- `user-stories-supplied.md`: the vendor's eight stories
- `requirements-checklist.md`: the six questions you score with
- `data-handling-policy.md`: what may and may not go into a prompt
- `scaffold-stories.md`: the direction you complete and run
- `worksheet.md`: your answers

## Tools

Claude Code in the workspace, for Part D.
