# Cedar Clinic: data in AI prompts

Staff and contractors may use the approved AI assistant for planning, requirements, and design
work. This page says what may go into a prompt. It applies to text typed, pasted, or attached.

## Never in a prompt

- A patient's name, date of birth, phone number, email address, or address.
- A reason for visit, diagnosis, medication, or any other health detail about a person.
- Anything copied from a patient record, an appointment record, a message to or from a patient, or a
  bill, even with the name removed.
- Passwords, keys, or anything that grants access to a clinic system.

## Allowed in a prompt

- How the clinic works: locations, number of providers, hours, the rules for booking and cancelling.
- Role names: patient, front-desk staff, administrator, provider.
- The kind of data a system holds, described in general: "a patient's date of birth", not a date.
- Synthetic patients: made-up people with made-up details, labeled as such ("Patient A", "a patient
  born in 1980").
- The scenario brief and these rules.

## When unsure

Leave it out and describe the shape of the data instead: "the patient's phone number on file", not
a number. Questions go to the practice manager. Nobody else approves exceptions.
