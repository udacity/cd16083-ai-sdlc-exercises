# The rules, written before any stories

Four acceptance criteria the business already knows. Each came from the team, not from the assistant, and each goes into the prompt as a rule.

1. **Cancellation and payment.** A session can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before it starts. After that, the student is not refunded and the tutor is still paid. *(Finance and the tutor agreement.)*
2. **Local time.** Every session time is shown in each person's own local time. *(Support: most missed sessions were time-zone mix-ups.)*
3. **Who gets the messages.** Confirmations and reminders go to whoever booked. For a student under 18, that is the parent, by email or text message. *(Support: many younger students have no email address.)*
4. **Moving a session.** A session can be moved to another time with the same tutor up to 24 hours before it starts. *(Product: the most common request after "cancel".)*

## Example story, in the shape we want

**Story: Cancel a session.** As a student, I want to cancel a booked session, so that I am not charged for a session I cannot attend.

- Cancelling 24 hours or more before the start refunds the student in full and frees the slot.
- Cancelling less than 24 hours before the start gives no refund, and the tutor is paid for the session.
- The cancellation notice goes to whoever booked, by their chosen method.
