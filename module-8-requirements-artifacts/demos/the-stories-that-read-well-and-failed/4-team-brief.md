# Scenario brief: what the team knows

Atlas is an online tutoring marketplace. Students anywhere book one-hour video sessions with tutors anywhere.

## Who uses it

- **Students**, who book and attend sessions. Tutors and students are often in different time zones.
- **Parents or guardians**, who book for students under 18. Many younger students have no email address of their own.
- **Tutors**, who set their availability and are paid for each session.

## Rules

1. Every session time is shown in each person's own local time.
2. A session can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before it starts. After that, the tutor is still paid.
3. A session can be moved to another time with the same tutor up to 24 hours before it starts.
4. Confirmations and reminders go to whoever booked. For a student under 18, that is the parent, by email or text message.
5. Tutors are paid weekly for every session that was not cancelled in time.

## Existing systems

- A payments provider charges students and pays tutors weekly.
- A video service hosts the sessions.
